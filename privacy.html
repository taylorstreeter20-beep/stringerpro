Privacy Policy

Stringer Pro · Effective October 8, 2026

Stringer Pro is a fishing app for anglers on the public waters of Illinois, Iowa and Missouri, published by Taylor Streeter of DeSoto, Illinois (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

You can use the app without an account

Browsing the map and every water, statewide limits, stocking reports, weather, the species guide and state records works without signing up. As a guest you give us no name, email or password, and nothing you look at is tied to an identity.

An account (email address and password) is needed only for the features that store your own content: the catch log, groups, the group feed, the leaderboard and your profile. A few things happen in the background whether or not you are signed in — weather requests, app updates, crash reports and, if you subscribe, the subscription check. Each is described below.

What we collect when you have an account

  • Account: your email address, the display name you choose and, if you add one, a profile photo. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored only in hashed form — we never see it.
  • Catches you log: species, length, optional weight, the water you tagged, the date and time, an optional photo, and — if you allow location access when you log it — the GPS coordinates where you logged the catch.
  • A conditions snapshot saved with each catch: air temperature, wind, barometric pressure and its trend, sky and moon phase, so Your Patterns can show when you fish best.
  • Groups: the private groups you create or join and their names.
  • Comments and reactions you post on catches in your groups.
  • Reports and blocks: any report you file about a catch, comment or person (what you reported, the reason and any note you add) and the list of people you have blocked.
  • App activity: a count of how many times your account opens the app each day, used only to see how many anglers use the app. No screens, taps, searches or locations are recorded.
  • Crash reports: if the app hits an error it sends the error message, a technical stack trace, the app version and your device's operating system and version. When you are signed in the report is linked to your account so we can fix your problem; as a guest it is not linked to anyone.

Location

  • Stringer Pro asks for location only while you are using the app. It never tracks you in the background.
  • Finding waters, stocking and Best Water picks near you, and centering the map on you, are calculated on your phone — that location is not sent to us. The blue “you are here” dot is drawn by Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps (Android).
  • When you log a catch with location on, the coordinates are saved with the catch, and the app sends them to Open-Meteo rounded to four decimal places (about 11 meters) to look up the weather at that moment. Open-Meteo receives no account information.
  • Everywhere else in the app, weather is requested for the location of the water you are looking at, not your own.
  • Location is optional. You can log a catch without it and turn it off for Stringer Pro at any time in your device settings.

Who can see what you post

There is no public feed. Your catches, photos, comments, reactions and profile are visible to you and to the members of the private groups you belong to, and group leaderboards show your display name and points to those members.

Group-mates' apps receive the exact coordinates saved with your catches, so the members of your groups can see where each catch was logged. If you don't want a spot known, log that catch with location turned off.

Catch and profile photos are stored with our hosting provider and shown in the app through short-lived links.

If you block someone, the app hides their catches and profile from you — and yours from them — without removing either of you from a group.

Service providers and other parties

We use a small number of services to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage provider, with servers in the United States. It stores the account data described above, and its servers log technical details such as IP addresses when the app connects.
  • Apple — sells and bills Stringer Pro subscriptions through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card or bank details.
  • RevenueCat — the subscription service that verifies purchases and tells the app whether Pro is unlocked. It receives an anonymous app user ID (or your account ID when you are signed in) and your App Store purchase information, such as the product, purchase and expiry dates, trial status and transaction identifiers. It receives no payment card data.
  • Expo (EAS Update) — the service that delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update it sends your IP address, your device platform and a random identifier for this installation.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts, as described under Location.
  • Public agency sources the app reads live: the U.S. Geological Survey (river gauge readings, requested by gauge number), the Illinois Department of Natural Resources (stocking reports) and the Iowa Department of Natural Resources (a water's Fish Iowa page). These requests carry nothing about you beyond what any web request does, such as your IP address. Missouri Department of Conservation data is built into the app.
  • Map imagery comes from Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android, which receive the map area being shown under their own privacy policies. Agency and source links open in your web browser.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal data or share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics or advertising SDK, and no advertising identifier in the app, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your contacts, browsing history or data from other apps.

Subscriptions

Stringer Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We and RevenueCat receive only the purchase details listed above, so the app knows which features to unlock. You can subscribe, restore a purchase and manage your subscription without a Stringer Pro account; if you are signed in, the subscription is linked to your account ID.

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest, without an account.
  • Location, camera and photo access are optional; the app keeps working without them, and you can change them any time in your device settings.
  • Edit or delete any catch you have logged, and delete your own comments.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, catches, photos, comments, reactions and group memberships. Groups you own pass to another member. It cannot be undone.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Stringer Pro subscription — Apple bills it, so cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings. Deleting the app does not delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by email. We answer within 30 days, wherever you live.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, catches, photos, comments, reactions, group memberships, blocks, the reports you filed and your daily app-open counts are deleted; crash reports you sent are kept to help fix bugs but are no longer linked to you. Deleted data can remain in our hosting provider's routine backups for a limited time until they are overwritten.

Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, and database rules restrict each record to the people allowed to see it. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Children

Stringer Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Our website

This policy is also posted at stringerproapp.com. The website counts page views with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies or collect personal information. The app itself does not use GoatCounter.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, data requests or privacy concerns: email us. We reply within a few days.
